For the helper

Eight picture questions for restaurant or school cafeteria practice. Read aloud speaks the scene, labels, and feedback. Help the player match each drawing to its meaning. Speech depends on the browser and device. There is no timer or public score. Wrong choices stay available for learning and give a short hint; the player moves on after finding the safe choice. The last question teaches checking a task list (paper or an approved phone app) before asking for the next task.

Follow the workplace food-safety plan and the supervisor's instructions. For spills, protect the area and get help. For food allergies, ask the supervisor and follow the allergy plan. These drawings are practice cues, not official safety signs.

Practice only. No names or answers are sent.
